Dead or Alive

MIT BWSI Serious Games & AI · Jul 2026 – present · team of 4 · Project Lead

  • Led a 4-person team building a rescue-triage game where a driver with a fogged view decides who to save, guided by an AI advisor that can see more than the player.
  • Built the driver's view across three fog levels, backed by Unity-rendered scenes with per-scene metadata, plus the core game loop and mode picker.
  • Turned the game into a FastAPI web study platform with a scripted schedule that makes the AI wrong on exactly 1 in 4 labels, so every participant sees the same accuracy.
  • Timed every decision in the browser and wrote it to logs that are never overwritten, with 11 automated tests covering a full round end to end.
