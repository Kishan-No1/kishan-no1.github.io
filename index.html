About

I'm a high school junior in Fremont, California. I build machine-learning systems and test whether they hold up on real data, not just on the benchmark they were trained for.

My first paper, on getting crystal-structure classifiers trained on simulated X-ray data to work on real measurements, was accepted at IEEE ONCON 2026. At MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute I led a five-person team building a game that studies when people trust an AI advisor, and I'm now co-first author on the study that grew out of it. I'm also doing statistical methods research with Georgia Tech.

Outside research, I build things at hackathons (track winner at VikingHacks 2026), and I founded Python BAY Academy, which taught Python to about 75 middle schoolers. I'm looking for software engineering and machine learning internships for summer 2027.
