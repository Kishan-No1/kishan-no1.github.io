Bit Museum

Bit Museum turns a digital art collection into a place you walk through: an 8-bit museum with the feel of a community-driven RPG, where art isn't just viewed but explored. Instead of browsing room by room, a visitor can describe what they're looking for and get matching pieces.

I built the backend: a FastAPI service over the museum's art database, and the retrieval-augmented (RAG) agent that turns a visitor's description into a search over that database and returns the closest matches. The front end is Next.js, with Phaser.js running the 8-bit world.
